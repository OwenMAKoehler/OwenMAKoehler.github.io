About me

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at Northeastern University working to make robotics more accessible.

During my co-op at Abbott Heart Failure, I worked on LVAD systems, the heart pumps that keep patients alive while they wait for a transplant or when a transplant isn't an option. I also had the chance to meet heart failure patients who rely on these devices. Hearing them describe how the technology lets them play sports, travel, or simply get through the day changed how I think about engineering. It showed me that technology matters most when it reaches the people who need it, and that's what drives me to make robotics more affordable and accessible.
